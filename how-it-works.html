Your product → your warehouse

How It Works

Four clear steps from idea to your warehouse. No ticket systems, no hidden fees, no surprise materials. You see every factory, every sample, every report.

01

Brief & Quote

Tell us what you want to build — product category, MOQ, target price, compliance needs, any reference images. We get back to you within 48 hours with a side-by-side quote: factory price + our flat 3–8% fee.

  • ✓ No obligation, no deposit
  • ✓ Real 1688 / Yiwu market sourcing prices, not fabricated ones
  • ✓ MOQ from 1 carton — we tell you if it's realistic

02

Supplier Audit

Before we recommend any factory, our team walks in person and runs our 27-point on-site audit. Factory registration, fire safety, electrical certifications, QC staffing, export licensing — everything documented with photos.

  • ✓ Only audited factories — no random 1688 middlemen
  • ✓ Audit report shared before your deposit
  • ✓ IP agreement signed — your design, your mold

03

Sample & QC

Sample in 3–7 days. First production order: piece-by-piece inspection on every unit — you get a photo report before you pay the balance. Reorders: AQL 2.5 sampling inspection.

  • ✓ Photo report, not just text — you see every defect
  • ✓ If batch fails QC, we negotiate replacement or discount before shipping
  • ✓ Compliance support: CE, FDA, CPC, food-grade documentation

04

Prep & Ship

FBA prep, carton labeling, barcoding, customs documentation. We consolidate multiple suppliers into one container, handle DDP shipping, and give you the full arrival forecast.

  • ✓ Amazon carton spec checked to prevent receiving rejections
  • ✓ Consolidation = one container for 3+ suppliers, one customs entry
  • ✓ DDP to any Amazon FBA or your warehouse — you know the landed cost up front
