Quality Control & AQL 2.5

AQL 2.5 is the industry-standard acceptance quality limit for consumer goods. At that level we sample statistically and reject the batch if major defects exceed a set threshold. Every Milebond reorder runs an AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection, with a written report sent to you. Message us on WhatsApp to see a sample report.
Gate 1 is a pre-production sample test, where we confirm the sample matches your spec before bulk starts. Gate 2 is a bulk sample test on the first units off the line. Gate 3 is the AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection before the container closes. Request a free quote and we will map these gates to your order timeline.
