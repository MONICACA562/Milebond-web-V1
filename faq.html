MOQ & Small Order Sourcing

How we help startups and small businesses source with minimum order quantities from 1 carton.

It depends on the product and supplier:
  • Stock availability: We can accept your quantity if the factory has enough stock
  • Factory MOQ: New production usually requires meeting the factory's minimum order quantity
  • Combined orders: We may add your order to a larger client's production run, allowing smaller quantities
We'll work to find a solution for your desired order size.

Home/FAQ/🚚 Logistics & Shipping/Shipping Options & Costs

Shipping Options & Costs

Sea, air, EXW, FOB, CIF — all your shipping questions answered.

We offer both sea and air shipping to meet your needs:
  • Sea shipping: Economical for larger volumes (FCL or LCL)
  • EXW: You arrange pickup from our warehouse
  • FOB: Covers all costs to forward and load cargo on board
  • CIF: Covers all costs to the port of your destination
  • Air cargo: Faster for valuable, smaller items
Let our sourcing agent help you choose the best option for your business.
