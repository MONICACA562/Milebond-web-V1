By sending an inquiry or placing an order, you accept them. If you need a signed agreement, we are happy to work from your template or ours.

1. Who we are

Milebond is a sourcing, supplier verification and factory audit company operating from Yiwu City, Zhejiang Province, China. In these terms, “we” and “us” mean Milebond; “you” means the business buyer using our services. Contact: info@milebond.com.

2. Scope of services

  • Sourcing and shortlisting suppliers for the products you describe.
  • Price, MOQ and lead-time checks with those suppliers.
  • Sample coordination and consolidation of orders.
  • Supplier verification: a free desk check, and an on-site factory audit when you order one.
  • Production follow-up and pre-shipment inspection as agreed in writing.
  • Shipping coordination through the forwarder you choose or one we recommend.

We work as an independent sourcing partner. We are not the manufacturer and do not own the factories we assess; goods are supplied by third-party factories and their own warranties apply.

3. Quotes and pricing

Quotes are based on the information you provide and on the prices available from suppliers at that time. They are valid for the period stated on the quote. Product prices are exclusive of freight, insurance, duties, taxes and bank charges unless the quote says otherwise. If specifications, quantities or materials change, we will re-quote.

4. Orders and payment

An order becomes binding when you confirm it in writing and we issue a proforma invoice or order confirmation. Production normally starts after the deposit shown on that document has cleared. Payment terms, currencies and balances are those stated in the proforma invoice. Third-party bank charges are borne by the payer.

5. Samples

Sample costs, tooling costs and courier charges are payable by you unless we agree otherwise in writing. Samples are produced by the factory and may differ slightly from mass production because of normal manufacturing tolerances.

6. Lead times

Lead times are estimates given by the factory. They depend on material availability, factory capacity, customization and your approvals, and are not a guaranteed delivery date unless we confirm one in writing.

7. Shipping, delivery and risk

Delivery terms follow the Incoterms stated on the proforma invoice or service agreement. Risk in the goods passes as set out in that Incoterm. Import duties, taxes and destination clearance are your responsibility unless agreed otherwise. Where we coordinate freight, we do so on your behalf and the carrier’s terms apply.

8. Inspection and acceptance

Inspection scope, sampling plan and reporting format are those agreed for your order. Please raise any shortage, damage or quality claim promptly after delivery and before the goods are resold, altered or installed, so we can investigate with the factory while evidence is still available.

9. Supplier verification and factory audits

The desk check is free and results in a Pass / Not Pass view based on the documents and information available. An on-site audit is a paid service; you receive a written report with findings and photographs. Both are professional opinions formed at the time of the check or visit, based on the factory we saw and the evidence supplied. They are not a guarantee of a supplier’s future conduct, financial standing or product quality, and they do not replace your own commercial judgement.
