LEGAL · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Milebond is a China sourcing company based in Yiwu, Zhejiang. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use milebond.com or send us an inquiry, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to quotes, samples and orders arranged through it.

1. Who we are

Milebond (“we”, “us”) is a sourcing, supplier verification and factory audit company operating from Yiwu City, Zhejiang Province, China, and working with buyers worldwide since 2015. For any privacy question, contact info@milebond.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us. When you submit a quote or contact form, we receive the details you enter: name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, company name, product category, product description, target quantity, expected MOQ, the service you are interested in, and your message. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we keep that correspondence.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting and security provider records technical data needed to deliver the site and protect it from abuse: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page and timestamps.

We do not ask for payment card details through this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare quotes, options and timelines.
  • To match your request with suitable suppliers and factories.
  • To arrange samples, inspections, consolidation and shipping when you place an order.
  • To keep records of quotes, orders and after-sales issues.
  • To improve our website, catalog and service quality.
  • To comply with accounting, tax and other legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Legal bases (EEA and UK visitors)

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: steps taken at your request before entering a contract (preparing a quote); our legitimate interests in responding to business inquiries and improving our service; your consent where you ask to receive updates; and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Who we share information with

  • Form processing. Our website forms are delivered through a third-party form-processing service (Web3Forms), which transmits your submission to our mailbox.
  • Hosting and security. The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes technical request data as part of content delivery and security.
  • Communication providers. Email and WhatsApp providers that carry our correspondence with you.
  • Order fulfilment. When you ask us to quote or produce, the specific suppliers, inspection partners and freight forwarders involved in your request receive only the details needed to do that work.
  • Legal requirements. Authorities or advisers where we are legally required, or where needed to establish or defend a legal claim.

6. International transfers

We operate from China and use service providers with infrastructure in other regions, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers are covered by appropriate contractual safeguards with those providers.

7. How long we keep it

Inquiry records are kept for up to 24 months from our last contact, unless an active quote, order or legal obligation requires us to keep them longer. After that we delete or anonymise them.

8. Cookies and analytics

We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not embed third-party advertising pixels. Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary security cookies to keep the site available and protected. If we add analytics in future, we will update this page first.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, to receive a portable copy, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email info@milebond.com and we will respond within the period required by applicable law. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to inquiry data is limited to team members who need it to answer you or fulfil an order, and our service providers operate under contractual confidentiality obligations. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your information.

11. Children

This site is intended for business buyers and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The “last updated” date at the top of this page shows the current version.
