Solutions

For Small Businesses & Startups

You do not need a container to start. Test the product on a small batch, keep the cash, and only scale what sells — without being pushed into a factory's minimum.

Start with a small order See pricing

Built for a first order, not a first container

1 carton

Minimum order

Start from a single carton where the product allows it. If a factory MOQ is genuinely unrealistic for your plan, we say so instead of quietly inflating the order.

3–7 days

Sampling

See and touch the product before committing to production. Sample cost and lead time are quoted up front.

3–8%

One visible fee

No retainer to start, no hidden margin inside the unit price. The factory price and our fee are separate lines.

What we will not do

  • —Push you into a volume you cannot sell just to meet a supplier's minimum.
  • —Quote a single bundled price that hides the factory cost.
  • —Promise a timeline the factory has not confirmed in writing.

A small first order is a test of the supplier as much as the product. We keep the first batch small, inspect it properly, and let the sell-through decide what happens next.
