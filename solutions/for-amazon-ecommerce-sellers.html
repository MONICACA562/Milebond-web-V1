The real work

Sourcing Products Is Only One Part of Running an Amazon Business

Finding a supplier is often the first step. The real challenge is managing everything that happens before products reach your Amazon inventory.

Too Many Suppliers to Manage

Different products often come from different factories. Managing communication, production schedules, quality requirements and shipments across multiple suppliers can become time-consuming.

Uncertain Product Quality

A low quotation does not guarantee consistent product quality. Without proper inspection and supplier follow-up, quality problems may only become visible after goods arrive.

MOQ and Inventory Decisions

Ordering too little may create replenishment pressure. Ordering too much can tie up working capital and increase inventory risk.

Complicated Shipment Coordination

When products come from multiple factories, receiving, inspection, labeling, packaging and shipment coordination can become difficult to manage.

Replenishment Delays

Production and shipping take time. Poor purchasing decisions can leave sellers with insufficient inventory while waiting for the next shipment.

MOQ & purchasing

What Is the Right MOQ for an Amazon Product?

The right MOQ is not always the lowest MOQ. For Amazon sellers, purchasing decisions should balance demand, inventory investment, production lead time and replenishment risk.

In-Stock Products

For products available in supplier stock, sourcing can start from 1 piece. This can be useful for product evaluation, samples or small purchasing needs.

Made-to-Order Products

For products that require production, the typical MOQ starts at around 100 pieces, depending on the product, specifications and factory requirements.

Why the Lowest MOQ Is Not Always the Best Choice

Ordering a smaller quantity may reduce initial inventory investment, but it can also increase the risk of running out of stock before replenishment arrives.

Before deciding how much to order, consider your sales velocity, current inventory, production lead time, shipping time and safety stock.

Production lead time: typical production lead time is around 25–30 days, depending on the product and factory. Shipping time must be considered separately.

Quality control

How Does Milebond Control Product Quality?

Quality control should be part of the sourcing process, not something considered only after products arrive.

First Orders: 100% Full Inspection

We conduct 100% full inspection for first orders to check product quality against the confirmed requirements.

Repeat Orders: AQL 2.5

For repeat orders, we apply AQL 2.5 as the sampling acceptance standard.

Inspection requirements should be agreed according to the product, specifications and order requirements. AQL 2.5 is a sampling acceptance standard; it is not a 2.5% defect-rate promise.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About China Sourcing for Amazon Sellers

Can Milebond source products from China with an MOQ of 1 piece?

For products available in supplier stock, Milebond can support sourcing from 1 piece. For products that require new production, the MOQ is generally around 100 pieces, depending on the product and factory.

What is the typical MOQ for made-to-order products?

The typical MOQ starts at around 100 pieces, but the actual quantity depends on the product, specifications, materials and factory requirements.

Is the lowest MOQ always the best choice for Amazon sellers?

Not necessarily. A lower MOQ may reduce initial inventory investment, but it can increase replenishment risk. Amazon sellers should consider sales velocity, current stock, production lead time and shipping time before deciding how much to order.

How much should an Amazon seller order for the first purchase?

There is no universal quantity. The right order size depends on expected demand, sales history, inventory budget, production lead time and shipping schedule. We recommend evaluating these factors together rather than choosing a quantity based only on the lowest MOQ.

How long does production usually take?

Typical production lead time is around 25–30 days, depending on the product and factory. Shipping time is additional and should be included in your replenishment plan.

Can Milebond coordinate multiple factories and different SKUs?

Yes. Milebond has experience coordinating sourcing across product categories and managing multiple suppliers and SKUs. We can help organize supplier communication, production follow-up, goods receiving, inspection and shipment preparation.

Can products from different factories be consolidated into one shipment?

Consolidation may be possible when production schedules, product characteristics and shipping requirements allow. Whether goods can be combined depends on the specific order and logistics arrangements.

How does Milebond inspect product quality?
