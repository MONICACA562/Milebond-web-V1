QC & Factory Audit

A 3-stage QC pipeline runs on every batch, with a 27-point factory audit applied to every supplier.

1 · IQC — Incoming Material

Raw-material and component verification against spec: materials, dyes and non-toxic certification.

2 · IPQC — In-Line (4 Checkpoints)

Key dimensions, build quality, print / logo and assembly checked at 4 points along production.

Small-parts hazard check, function test and AQL 2.5 sampling. Photo + video report before balance payment.
