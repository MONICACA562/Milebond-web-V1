Sleeping Bag Compliance Requirements for US & EU Markets

Compliance requirements for sleeping bags depend on the destination market, product construction, intended use and sales channel. Some requirements apply to both online and offline sales, while others specifically govern online product listings or conditional chemical warnings.

Mandatory Conditional Voluntary / Recommended

Mandatory = required by applicable law. Conditional = mandatory when specified legal or market conditions apply. Voluntary / Recommended = not universally required by law but useful for product verification and buyer confidence.

1. United States — Online vs. Offline

RequirementStatusOffline / physical productOnline listing / advertisement
FTC Textile Fiber Labeling16 CFR Part 303 Mandatory Covered sleeping bags require physical product labels containing the applicable fiber content, responsible business identification and country-of-origin information. Online advertisements that make fiber claims must comply with applicable FTC advertising disclosure rules. Online catalogs must provide the applicable country-of-origin statement. An online listing does not replace the physical product label.
Country of OriginImported products Mandatory Apply the applicable country-of-origin marking required for imported products. Include the applicable origin statement in the online catalog, and keep it consistent with the product label.
California Proposition 65Chemical exposure warnings Conditional Conditional, not universally mandatory for every sleeping bag. Assess chemical exposure and applicable warning thresholds; where a warning is required, use an applicable product warning method. Where a warning is required, online purchases require an appropriate pre-purchase internet warning. Do not automatically add a warning to every product without assessment.
Thermal Performance TestingISO 23537-1 Voluntary Voluntary as a general US legal requirement. Recommended when making specific temperature-performance claims; keep a supporting test report for the supplied construction. Use test results to substantiate listing claims. Do not describe supplier temperature descriptions as independently certified ratings without a supporting report.
FlammabilityProduct classification Conditional Assess applicable product classification and any relevant state-specific requirements before making a flammability statement. Do not automatically claim that 16 CFR Part 1610 applies to every adult sleeping bag.
Listing does not replace labeling. An online catalog entry or advertisement supports the sale, but it does not replace the physical fiber label or country-of-origin marking on the product.

2. European Union — Online vs. Offline

RequirementStatusOffline / physical productOnline listing / offer
GPSRRegulation (EU) 2023/988 Mandatory Product safety requirements apply to online and offline consumer sales. Prepare a product risk assessment and technical documentation as applicable, and provide applicable manufacturer and EU responsible person information. Online product offers must visibly display the information required by GPSR Article 19, including manufacturer information, EU responsible person information where applicable, product identification and required warnings.
EU Responsible PersonGPSR identification Conditional Where the manufacturer is outside the EU, ensure the required EU responsible person is established and identified in accordance with the applicable rules. Provide the required identification on the product, packaging, parcel or accompanying documentation as applicable. Show the required EU responsible person contact details in the online offer.
REACHRegulation (EC) No 1907/2006 Mandatory Applicable chemical restrictions apply equally to online and offline products. Assess relevant fabrics, fillings, dyes, coatings, printing and components. Where the applicable SVHC threshold and conditions are met, explain the Article 33 communication obligation. The same restriction and communication obligations apply to the product offered online. Do not describe a single generic REACH certificate as universally mandatory for every sleeping bag.
EU Textile RegulationRegulation (EU) No 1007/2011 Mandatory Fibre composition labeling is mandatory for covered textile products. Consumer-facing product labels must meet applicable durability, legibility and language requirements. In certain B2B supply-chain transactions, accompanying commercial documents may replace or supplement labels as permitted by the Regulation. The consumer-facing offer must not be treated as an exemption from textile labeling. Do not confuse B2B document exceptions with consumer retail labeling requirements.
EN ISO 23537-1:2022Thermal performance Voluntary A voluntary technical standard, not a universal CE certification requirement. Recommended when making specific thermal performance claims, and temperature-rating care labelling should match the claim. Verify that the tested product corresponds to the actual supplied construction. Use a report for the matching construction to support temperature claims in the listing.
CE MarkingIntended use assessment Conditional Ordinary adult sleeping bags do not automatically require CE marking simply because they are sold in the EU. Assess the product's intended use and any other applicable legislation before making a CE-related claim. Do not add a CE claim or CE mark to an online listing without that assessment.
CE marking is not automatic. A CE mark is not a general sleeping-bag requirement; product classification and intended use determine whether any CE legislation applies.

3. Compliance Classification

Mandatory

Legal requirements that must be met for the relevant market and product.

Conditional

Requirements triggered by destination, materials, chemical exposure, intended use, product classification or sales channel.

Voluntary / Recommended

Additional tests and standards that are not universally required but can support product performance claims and commercial confidence.

4. Online vs. Offline: What Actually Changes?

Product safety and applicable chemical restrictions generally apply regardless of whether a sleeping bag is sold online or offline. Online sales create additional product-listing and pre-purchase information obligations in certain markets. Offline sales require appropriate physical labels and consumer-facing information. B2B supply-chain documentation exceptions must not be confused with consumer retail exemptions.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

What materials are used and is it safe?

Made with a 190T polyester shell and hollow-fibre fill, supplied with a storage bag and FTC fiber label. Materials are verified batch-by-batch on every order; QC checks incoming material, build and surface finish before shipment.

What variants are available?

What US and EU compliance documents do you support?

We support GPSR (EU) 2023/988 product-safety coordination; REACH (EC) 1907/2006 restricted-substance assessment; EU 1007/2011 fibre-composition labelling; and thermal-performance documentation. Available documents and items requiring supplier confirmation are identified at quotation, and test reports are shared on request where held.

Are sleeping bag compliance requirements different for online and offline sales?

Core product safety and applicable material requirements generally apply to both. Online sales may require additional product-page information and pre-purchase warnings.

Is a textile label required for sleeping bags sold in the US?

Yes. Sleeping bags are covered by applicable FTC textile labeling rules, subject to the specific requirements and exceptions.

Does every sleeping bag sold in California need a Proposition 65 warning?

No. It depends on applicable chemical exposure and warning requirements.

Does an adult sleeping bag need CE marking in the EU?

Not automatically. Ordinary adult sleeping bags do not require CE marking solely because they are sold in the EU.

Is EN ISO 23537-1 mandatory?

It is a voluntary technical standard in general, but is recommended for substantiating thermal performance claims.

Can B2B commercial documents replace textile labels in the EU?

In specified B2B supply-chain situations, Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011 permits accompanying commercial documents to replace or supplement labels. This should not be treated as a general exemption for consumer retail products.

How should it be stored and maintained?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep it clean and dry between uses; follow the care notes on the hangtag / label. QC verifies this labelling on every batch.
