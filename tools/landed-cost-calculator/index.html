HS codes

What we can suggest — and what only a customs classification can confirm

A product name can point you to a 6-digit HS heading: ceramic mugs to 6912.00, power banks to 8507.60, wooden furniture to 9403.60. That is useful for orientation.

It cannot reliably produce the 8–10 digit national code that sets the actual rate. The same “bag” can be 4202.21 (leather) or 4202.92 (textile), with materially different duty. The same “toy” can be 9503 (toys) or 4016 (rubber articles). National codes also change, and the US adds Section 301 duties on top of MFN rates for many Chinese goods.

So this tool suggests the HS6 heading and sends you to the official tariff. The duty field stays editable, and every Milebond quote confirms the classification in writing before you order.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask before pricing

Is this an Amazon FBA fee calculator?

It models the Amazon costs that decide margin — referral fee, fulfilment, advertising, returns and storage — using the rates you enter. Amazon's actual fees depend on category, size tier and weight, so check Seller Central for your exact SKU.

Can it classify my product automatically?

It suggests the 6-digit HS heading from a product name. It does not assign the national 8–10 digit code, because that depends on material, construction and end use, and it carries legal responsibility. We confirm the classification in every quote.

Is duty charged on FOB or CIF?

In most markets duty is charged on CIF: goods plus international freight plus insurance. Expensive freight therefore increases duty, which is why a low unit price can still produce a high landed cost.

Do I pay VAT if I am not registered?

Yes. VAT or GST is collected at import whether or not you are registered. Registration only determines whether you can recover it later.

What counts as a good margin?

For Amazon or DTC, 20%+ net is healthy, 10–20% leaves little room for price wars and returns, below 10% rarely survives advertising and cash-flow costs. Offline wholesale depends on volume and payment terms.

Can you confirm the numbers before I order?

Yes. Send the product, quantity and destination and we return an itemized quote with goods, freight, duty, our fee and the landed cost per unit — within 24 hours.
