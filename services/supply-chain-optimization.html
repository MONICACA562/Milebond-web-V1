5 Steps to a Leaner, Faster Supply Chain

A systematic approach to uncovering inefficiencies and building a resilient supply network

01

Supply Chain Audit

We map your entire supply chain — from raw material to last-mile delivery — identifying bottlenecks, cost leaks, and risk points across every tier.

02

Supplier Benchmarking

We evaluate your existing suppliers against 50+ criteria (cost, quality, lead time, compliance) and identify alternative suppliers for leverage.

03

Optimization Strategy

We deliver a prioritized action plan — renegotiation targets, consolidation opportunities, logistics rerouting, and supplier diversification recommendations.

04

Implementation & Transition

We execute the plan: renegotiate contracts, onboard new suppliers, reroute logistics, and manage transitions without disrupting your operations.

05

Continuous Monitoring

We track KPIs monthly — cost per unit, on-time delivery, defect rates — and proactively adjust the strategy as markets and suppliers evolve.

How an Amazon FBA Brand Cut Sourcing Costs by 27% and Reduced Lead Times by 35 Days

A mid-sized Amazon seller with 120+ SKUs across home goods and kitchenware was struggling with rising costs and inconsistent quality. Their supply chain was fragmented across 18 suppliers with no leverage or oversight.

We conducted a full audit, benchmarked all 18 suppliers, consolidated the supply base to 9 strategic partners, renegotiated volume-based pricing, rerouted logistics through our Yiwu consolidation hub, and implemented pre-production quality checks. Within 6 months, per-unit costs dropped 27%, lead times shortened from 65 to 30 days, and defect rates fell from 8% to under 1.5%.

FAQ About Supply Chain Optimization

Everything you need to know about optimizing your China supply chain

Standard sourcing focuses on finding and managing suppliers for specific products. Supply chain optimization is a strategic, holistic service that examines your entire supply network — including existing suppliers, logistics, warehousing, and inventory — to identify systemic improvements. It's ideal for businesses already sourcing from China that want to reduce costs, improve reliability, and build resilience.
Supply chain optimization delivers value at any scale:

Small businesses (10–50 SKUs): Even small inefficiencies compound. We help you avoid overpaying, consolidate orders for volume discounts, and set up scalable processes early.

Mid-size businesses (50–500 SKUs): This is where optimization has the biggest impact — consolidating fragmented suppliers, negotiating better terms, and streamlining logistics.

Large businesses (500+ SKUs): Complex multi-tier supply chains benefit from systematic risk management, KPI-driven supplier management, and advanced logistics optimization.
The initial audit and strategy phase typically takes 2–4 weeks, depending on the complexity of your supply chain. Implementation is phased over 1–3 months to avoid disrupting your operations. Continuous monitoring is ongoing. Most clients see measurable cost reductions within the first quarter.
