8-Step Development Process

How We Turn Ideas Into Best-Selling Products

Our systematic 8-step approach takes you from raw concept to production-ready product, de-risking every decision with data.

1

Market Research & Opportunity Analysis

Red ocean vs blue ocean analysis, competitor benchmarking, gap identification. We map the landscape before you commit.

2

Customer Review Mining

Scrape negative reviews across Amazon, eBay, Trustpilot and niche forums. Find the exact pain points customers are complaining about.

3

Product Concept & Improvement Proposals

Translate review insights into concrete improvement suggestions. Clear, actionable ideas that make your product meaningfully better.

4

Bundle & Upsell Strategy

Propose bundle ideas and upsell combinations with mockup kits. Raise AOV and create perceived value that competitors don't offer.

5

Component Quality Upgrade

Upgrade critical small components — handles, clasps, seals, coatings — that customers notice and praise in reviews.

6

Packaging Optimization

Optimize packaging for lower cost, better product protection, and smaller shipping volume. Every gram saved adds to your margin.

7

Landed Cost Analysis

Include customs clearance, duty and logistics in your budget. We give you hard numbers from supply-chain perspective — no hidden surprises.

8

Go/No-Go Decision Support

Step back and evaluate the whole picture. Is customization truly worth it? We give you an honest, data-backed recommendation before you spend.

Case Study

Real Results: Idea to Best-Seller in 6 Weeks

A European e-commerce seller shared how our 8-step process took their concept to a top-performing SKU.

European Kitchenware Brand — Custom Silicone Baking Mat Set

The client arrived with a product concept and a custom logo. We ran the full 8-step development process — from red/blue ocean analysis through customer review mining on Amazon.de, Amazon.co.uk and Trustpilot. We identified a gap: all competitors cut corners on the small edge flaps and non-slip texture in the corners.

Our team proposed upgraded materials for those specific components, optimized the packaging to reduce shipment volume by 32%, and built a mockup bundle with a free silicone spatula. The client approved the Go/No-Go recommendation, went into production, and launched in 6 weeks.
