Inspection Workflow

How Our Quality Control Works

Five-stage inspection woven into every production milestone, not a one-time afterthought.

1

Pre-production Inspection

Verify raw material quality, confirm pre-production sample, lock specification before mass production starts.

2

First-Article Inspection

Full check of first finished units to confirm function, dimension, appearance and packaging.

3

During-Production Inspection

On-site check when production reaches 20% progress, catch process drift early.

4

Final Random Inspection

Pre-shipment random inspection strictly following AQL 2.5 sampling rules.

5

Loading Supervision

Supervise container loading, prevent mixing-up, damage or substitution of goods.

Deliverables

What You Get

Every inspection delivers actionable results you can trust.

Multi-stage inspection reports with photos & defect records

Every stage of inspection is documented with high-resolution photos and detailed defect logs, so you can see exactly what is happening in your production line.

Strict AQL 2.5 sampling standard for finished goods

We follow the internationally recognized AQL 2.5 standard to determine sample sizes and acceptable defect limits for each inspection lot.

Quarterly supplier performance scoring

We track and score every supplier on quality, lead-time and responsiveness, so you know which suppliers are worth keeping long-term.

Non-conformity correction suggestions

When quality risk appears, we don't just report the problem — we provide actionable correction suggestions to bring production back on track.
