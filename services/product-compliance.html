EU Compliance Standards

Four Key Certifications You Need to Know

Each regulation applies to different product categories. Get it right before your shipment leaves the port.

Mark of Safety
CE

CE Marking

What Products Need It

Any product sold in the European Economic Area (EEA) that falls under one of 20+ EU New Approach Directives: toys, machinery, medical devices, electrical equipment, PPE, construction products, and more.

Key Points

  • Not a quality mark — declares conformity with EU safety, health, and environmental requirements
  • Manufacturer is legally responsible for compliance, even if subcontracted
  • Required on product, packaging, and technical documentation
  • Some categories require third-party Notified Body assessment
Common Myth

"CE on the product means it's certified." — Fake CE marks are widespread. A valid CE mark must be traceable to a real Technical File and Declaration of Conformity (DoC).

Hazardous Substances
🔌

RoHS (Restriction of Hazardous Substances)

Scope

Applies to electrical and electronic equipment (EEE) sold in the EU — from smartphones to washing machines, toys with batteries, LED bulbs, and cables.

Key Points

  • Restricts 10 hazardous substances: lead, mercury, cadmium, chromium VI, PBBs, PBDEs, DEHP, BBP, DBP, DIBP
  • Each homogeneous material in the product must comply individually
  • RoHS 2.0 (2011/65/EU) is currently the enforced directive
  • Declaration of Conformity must be issued by the manufacturer
Common Myth

"RoHS only applies to the product itself." — Components inside (batteries, wires, circuit boards, plastic parts) must also comply individually.

Chemicals Regulation
🧪

REACH (Registration, Evaluation, Authorization, and Restriction of Chemicals)

Scope

Covers all chemical substances manufactured or imported into the EU at 1 tonne or more per year. Also requires SVHC (Substances of Very High Concern) disclosure in articles.

Key Points

  • If your product contains 0.1% w/w of any SVHC from the candidate list, you must notify ECHA
  • Currently over 240 SVHCs on the candidate list — updated twice yearly
  • Suppliers must provide a SDS (Safety Data Sheet) for chemical substances
  • Importers are responsible for registration if EU-based
Common Myth

"REACH only applies to chemical products." — REACH applies to articles too, including furniture, textiles, and electronics that contain SVHCs.

Post-Brexit UK
🇬🇧

UKCA Marking

Scope

Replaced CE marking for products sold in Great Britain (England, Scotland, Wales) after Brexit. Northern Ireland follows EU rules and keeps CE marking.

Key Points

  • Applies to the same product categories that required CE marking in the EU
  • UK-based Authorized Representative may be needed if manufacturer is outside UK
  • UK conformity assessment bodies (not EU Notified Bodies) must be used for third-party assessment
  • Technical documentation must be kept for 10 years
Common Myth

"CE marking is still valid in the UK." — Since 1 January 2025, CE marking alone is NO longer recognized in Great Britain. You need the UKCA mark for full market access.
