The short version

Supplier verification is the process of confirming that a Chinese factory is who it claims to be — legally registered, capable of producing at your volume, and not a trading company posing as a factory. Alibaba "verified supplier" badges are paid, not audited. We do our own verification.

Our process has two stages: desk checks (business license, banking records, export history, online reputation) and a 27-point on-site audit where we visit the factory, walk the production floor, and verify capacity, quality systems and export experience in person.

We've placed orders with 6,900+ suppliers since 2015. That means we know which factories are reliable — and which to avoid.

Definitions

Supplier verification and factory audit: what each one is and what it does

What is Supplier Verification?

Supplier Verification is a remote desk check that confirms a supplier is legally registered, is who it claims to be, and is a genuine factory rather than a trading company posing as one. It checks the business license, unified social credit code, legal representative, registered capital, business scope, registration status, penalties and tax records, then cross-checks judicial and operational risk on independent platforms. It does not visit the factory. It is fast (usually 1-3 days), low-cost, and produces a Pass / Not Pass verdict plus a supplier profile you can act on.

What is a Factory Audit?

A Factory Audit is an on-site inspection of the physical factory. A Milebond auditor visits the site and verifies 27 specific points across six groups: legal entity, production capacity and people, quality system, on-site observation (lines, QC, warehouse, sample room), outsourcing and subcontractors, and commercial terms. Each factory is then scored on eight dimensions — Legal Qualification, Equipment & Technology, QC Management, Labor Skills & Export Experience, Production & Delivery Capacity, Document Management, Customer Service & Cooperation, New Product Development — with an A/B/C/D rating and a written report with photos. It is the professional check used before large orders, molds, private label or FBA first orders.

Who Needs Which Check

Who needs Supplier Verification — and who needs a Factory Audit

Choose Supplier Verification if you…

  • Are comparing multiple suppliers before your first quote
  • Buy small first orders or single products
  • Have a shortlist but haven't committed
  • Need to quickly filter out shell companies and fake factories
  • Want a free, fast sanity check before spending more.

Choose a Factory Audit if you…

  • Are placing a large or repeat order
  • Are starting a private label or custom mold (tooling)
  • Preparing an Amazon FBA first order
  • Need documented proof for your investors, insurer or team
  • Want to hold the factory accountable with a written 27-point report.

Supplier Verification · Desk Check

Three layers of checks — before we ever make the call.

Anyone can copy a website. It takes 10+ years and thousands of suppliers to read a business license the way we do. Here is what gets verified before a factory even enters your shortlist.

Layer 1 — Official Records (free, authoritative, first pass)

  • Business license & unified social credit code
  • Company name & legal representative
  • Registered capital & subscribed capital
  • Establishment date & operating term
  • Business scope & registration status
  • Shareholders & key personnel
  • Administrative penalties, business-exception list & serious-dishonesty list
  • Industry classification
  • Dual-disclosure licensing & penalties, tax blacklist, tax credit rating, court-ordered joint disciplinary action

Layer 2 — Third-Party Cross-Check (independent screening)

  • Business registration & shareholder records re-verified on independent platforms
  • Judicial risk — litigation & enforcement
  • Operational risk signals
  • Intellectual property & qualifications
  • Bidding / tender history

Layer 3 — Milebond Experience Judgment (what databases can't tell you)

  • Business-scope analysis: specialist manufacturer, or a broad reseller listing?
  • Location analysis: real production site, or a mailing address with no production capability?
  • Geography-driven cost analysis: labor cost, raw-material access, logistics proximity, or machinery & tooling?
The Milebond edge: 10+ years and thousands of suppliers taught us to read what a license doesn't say — whether the address is a factory or a mailbox, whether the scope says maker or reseller, and which cost advantage the location actually buys.

How We Score It

8 dimensions · A/B/C/D rating — the standard we actually use

Every audited factory gets scored on eight dimensions, exactly as in our internal audit report template. A = excellent · B = good · C = poor · D = terrible.

01

Legal Qualification

Business license, consistency of the production entity and legal person.

02

Equipment & Technology

Equipment to make your product, material & process knowledge, sample-making facilities.

03

Quality Control Management

Independent QC organization, approved sample & spec at production site, defect records & improvement.

04

Labor Skills & Export Experience

Experience making similar products and shipping to USA/Europe, samples proving the skill.

05

Production & Delivery Capacity

Ample production lines, storage space, efficiently organized to move goods.

06

Document Management

Documented, controlled and updated specs, POs and records.

07

Customer Service & Cooperation

Willingness to negotiate, grow with your business, arrange samples and visits.

08

New Product Development

Own core technology, patents, design review, tooling capability.

FACTORY AUDIT · CHECKLIST

60+ questions, 7 sections — the full factory visit checklist.

We don't walk into a factory and wing it. Every initial visit follows the same 60+ question checklist, split into seven sections. This is what separates a professional audit from a friendly tour.

Company Overview & Stability

Who we're actually dealing with

  • Company legal name
  • Years in business
  • Parent company or subsidiaries
  • Do you rent or own this factory building?

Financial & Commercial Terms

Can they stay in business and invoice properly

  • Audited financial statements for the last 2–3 years
  • Pricing model & structure breakdown
  • Volume discounts or long-term incentives
  • Warranties and guarantees

Products & Services

Can they make what you need

  • Lead time for standard and custom orders
  • Minimum order quantity (MOQ)
  • Product customization extent
  • R&D capability and product roadmap

Quality Management & Certifications

What proof do they actually have

  • Quality certifications held
  • Defect handling process
  • Standard quality metrics (defect rate, PPM, OTD%)
  • In-house testing capability and lab tests

Certificates are claims. We verify them on-site.

Production & Operational Capabilities

Can they deliver at your volume

  • Number of production lines
  • Annual capacity and current utilization
  • Machinery brand & model for key processes
  • Outsourced parts; nearest port & lead time

Capacity on paper vs capacity on the floor — we check both.

Compliance & Data Security

Do they clear the legal bar

  • Social compliance & supply chain security certs
  • Product safety and compliance documentation
  • Environmental (ISO 14001) and H&S (ISO 45001)
  • Code of conduct for labor and human rights

References & Customers

Who else already risks money on them

  • 3–5 current client references
  • Top 5 largest customers and regions
  • Revenue concentration of top three customers

The strongest evidence is who already bets on them.
