SUPPLIER VERIFICATION · FACTORY AUDIT

Supplier verification vs factory audit: which one do you need?

Supplier verification is free and remote. We research the supplier and give you a verdict within 24 hours on whether the factory is worth pursuing. A factory audit is paid and on-site — an auditor visits the factory, verifies 27 points and scores it A–D. Audits start at $250/day in Zhejiang and $350/day in Guangdong.

Supplier verification: a free judgment on whether to keep talking

This is not a database lookup. It is a short, honest assessment built from public records, trade contacts and 10+ years of evaluating Chinese suppliers.

What we check

  • Official records — business license, unified social credit code, legal representative, registered capital, business scope, operating status and penalties
  • Trade and market cross-checks — whether the address is a real production site, whether the product line matches the claimed category, and what buyers and agents in the same market say
  • Our own judgment — how the supplier compares with the 6,900+ factories we have worked with, and the specific risks we would negotiate before any deposit

What you get

  • A clear Pass / Not Pass verdict — not a data dump
  • The red flags we found, in plain language
  • The questions to ask and the terms to negotiate next
  • A recommendation: negotiate, audit, or walk away

Boundary: verification is remote. It cannot confirm that the line is running, that capacity is real, or that quality is stable. That is what the audit is for.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask before booking

Is supplier verification really free?

Yes. The remote check costs nothing and carries no obligation. We use it to decide whether a supplier is worth a deeper look — and to show how we work before you commit to anything.

What is the difference between verification and an audit?

Verification is remote, free and fast: public records, trade references and our own supplier history, ending in a Pass / Not Pass verdict. An audit is paid and on-site: an auditor verifies 27 points, scores the factory A–D and delivers a written report with photos. Verification filters; an audit documents.

How long does each take?

Verification comes back within 24 hours. An audit takes 1–2 days on site depending on factory size, with the written report delivered in 5–7 business days.

How much does a factory audit cost?

$250 per auditor per day in Zhejiang (Yiwu, Jinhua, Ningbo, Hangzhou) and $350 per day in Guangdong (Shenzhen, Dongguan, Guangzhou, Foshan). Other provinces are $350 per day plus travel at cost. ISO 9001, BSCI and SMETA scopes are quoted per scope.

Can verification replace an audit?

No. Verification tells you whether a supplier is worth pursuing. It cannot confirm the production line, real capacity or current quality. If the order is large, involves tooling or private label, or is your first FBA shipment, audit the factory.

Do you audit factories you did not source?

Yes. Most audits are for factories the buyer found independently — on Alibaba, at a trade fair, or through another agent. We have no incentive to favor a supplier we did not introduce.
