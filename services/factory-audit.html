Why It Matters

Why Factory Audit Is Non-Negotiable

Most overseas buyers discover too late that their "factory" isn't a factory at all.

Many "factories" you find online are actually trading companies. They mark up prices 20-50%, have no quality control, and disappear when problems arise.

Even real factories often exaggerate their capacity, certifications and experience. A factory audit is the only way to verify the truth before you place an order.

Our auditors visit the factory in person, check every critical area, and deliver a photo-rich report within 48 hours. We've audited 6,900+ suppliers and know exactly what to look for.

The Checklist

Our 27-Point Audit Checklist

Five categories, 27 verification points — no factory passes our audit without being measured against every single one.

① Basic Qualification (5 points)

  • Business license verification
  • Production permit check
  • Export qualification
  • Registered capital
  • Years in operation

② Production Capacity (6 points)

  • Factory area & ownership
  • Number of production lines
  • Equipment list & condition
  • Actual monthly output
  • Raw material storage
  • Finished goods warehouse

③ Quality System (7 points)

  • ISO certification check
  • Incoming material inspection
  • In-process QC procedures
  • Final inspection (AQL standard)
  • Defect handling & corrective action
  • Traceability system
  • QC lab equipment & personnel

④ Compliance & Social Responsibility (5 points)

  • Environmental permit
  • Fire safety inspection
  • Labor contract & conditions
  • Working hours & overtime
  • Child labor check

⑤ Financial & Risk (4 points)

  • Ownership structure verification
  • Litigation records check
  • Customer concentration risk
  • Payment risk assessment

Grading System

Factory Grading System

Every audited supplier receives a clear A/B/C/D grade based on their 27-point score.

A
Excellent
Fully qualified, recommended for direct partnership. Passes all 27 points with no major issues. Priority supplier for long-term collaboration.
B
Good
Qualified with minor issues. Recommended with improvement plan. Address minor issues within 30 days to maintain qualification.
C
Conditional
Has notable issues in capacity, quality or compliance. Only for low-risk, small-volume orders with strict supervision and clear milestones.
D
Not Recommended
Major issues or trader misrepresentation. Do not place orders. We will recommend alternative suppliers from our vetted database.
