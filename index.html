Operational foundation

Warehouse Operations, Multi-Supplier Consolidation & Kitting

Milebond combines upstream sourcing and supplier coordination with downstream warehouse operations. Unlike an assembly-only provider, we can also coordinate product sourcing, factory communication and production follow-up. Customers can work with their existing suppliers or ask Milebond to manage sourcing and supplier coordination.

Supplier sourcing and coordination → Factory production follow-up → Warehouse receiving → Quantity and condition checking → Quality inspection → Component organization → Kitting and assembly where required → Packaging and labeling → Shipment consolidation → Export shipment coordination

Model A

Customer-managed suppliers

Customers retain their existing supplier relationships. Suppliers deliver products or components to the agreed warehouse location. Milebond coordinates the agreed receiving, checking, inspection, assembly, packaging and outbound operations.

Model B

Milebond-managed suppliers

Milebond helps source and coordinate factories, manage purchase orders and supplier communication, follow production, arrange receiving and inspection, and coordinate downstream warehouse and shipment operations.

Multi-factory sourcing and coordination Inbound receiving and quantity checks Component quality inspection Assembly to agreed specifications Labeling and packaging Consolidated shipment Amazon FBA or other destination preparation where applicable DDP shipping where agreed for the destination

Customers do not need to purchase kitting or assembly services if they only require product sourcing or bulk procurement.

A China Warehouse Behind Your Sourcing Operations

Milebond operates warehouse infrastructure exceeding 1,000 square meters in the Yiwu–Jinhua area, supporting receiving, quantity checking, product and packaging inspection, component organization, kitting, packaging and shipment consolidation.

Consolidation storage is free for 20 calendar days from receipt. Extended storage is quoted by CBM, pallet or carton and approved before it accrues. Inspection, packaging and assembly are agreed per project, quoted separately where they fall outside the Managed Order service fee, and are not unlimited.
