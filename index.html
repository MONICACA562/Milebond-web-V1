Our story

We didn't start as a trading company. We started as a factory.

Monica Zhu ran a small packaging workshop before 2016. We incorporated to serve e-commerce brand owners, and slowly built the supply chain, team and experience to work with international buyers directly. Here's how that went.

Before 2016

The packaging workshop

Monica ran a small factory making packaging and paper goods for domestic e-commerce sellers. We learned production from the floor up — printing, die-cutting, folding, gluing. We knew what a real factory could and couldn't do, because we were one.

2016

First brand clients

We incorporated a domestic company and started taking orders from e-commerce brand owners — packaging, paper goods, and small assortments. We learned labeling, prep, and the margin math that makes or breaks a new brand's first product.

2016–2023

From packaging to full sourcing

Clients kept asking us to find other categories too — not just packaging. We built out a vetted supplier network across Yiwu, Jinhua and Guangzhou. E-commerce brands like Juvale have been our sourcing and shipping clients since their very first order — and we still work together today. We failed on enough shipments along the way to know where the landmines are.

2024–2025

Building the team and the floor

We hired dedicated sourcing, compliance, QC and shipping specialists. We built our own repacking and assembly line in the 800㎡ Jinhua warehouse, an hour from Yiwu — so kitting, polybagging, barcode labeling and assembly happen under our roof, not at a random subcontractor.

Now

Milebond — working directly with you

We launched Milebond to work directly with international brand owners. We waited until now because we didn't want to take your order and figure it out as we went. The team, the supply base, the warehouse and the scars — all in place.

Buyers ask us these. Here are honest answers.

How we actually work.

No sales pitch. Just how we run quotes and QC — the parts most agents leave out.

Why is your MOQ so low?

We run our own printing press and embossing equipment in Yiwu, with dedicated staff. Long-term partner label and box factories are on call. That means the "factory minimum" isn't always the real floor — we finish, label and pack small runs in-house without a separate setup fee.

How do you keep quality consistent?

Non-fragile goods: we take delivery at our warehouse, inspect every unit one by one, then repack. Fragile goods: we're on the factory floor before final packaging — in-line inspection, not a box-check at loading. You get dated photos from both stages.

Are you the cheapest quote?

No. If a cheaper price exists, we'll tell you exactly where it comes from — thinner board, offset vs digital print, skipped QC, slower shipping — so you choose the trade-off with eyes open. Sometimes cheaper is right for a trial order. Sometimes it isn't. Your call.

Can't find the exact product? Then what?

If we don't have the supplier, we say so — and if you really want it, we'll go source it ourselves, even if it doesn't become an order. We ask why you need that feature, then find the lowest-MOQ way to get the same result instead of pushing you into a new mold.

Why aren't you pushing me to order?

Because we're not here to move inventory. On a recent quote we talked a client out of a foldable basket (it still exceeded standard parcel oversize limits), out of a full-color gift box (+$1, 500 MOQ for a 250-unit trial), and out of a PVC sign (mold cost too high — MDF was better). We'd rather spend two extra weeks nailing the spec so your product wins on your channel. Your reorder matters more than your first deposit.

What we don't do

Most agents tell you what they can do. Here's what we won't.

— We don't pretend to be a 20-year company. Milebond is a new company. The 11+ years, the 6,900 suppliers, the long-term Juvale partnership — that's our team's track record, not this company's registration date. We say it plainly because due diligence will find out anyway.

— We don't mark up the factory invoice. Our 3–8% fee is its own line. The forwarder's bill passes through at cost. What the shipping company charges is what you pay.

— We don't say yes to every product. If it's outside what we know deeply — packaging, paper goods, and fulfillment prep — we tell you, and point you to someone who does it better.

— We don't push you into a 1,000-unit first order. Start at 250, prove the product, reorder. Dead stock hurts your business more than our smaller commission helps ours.

— We don't invent testimonials. No case study for your category yet? We'll say so. We'd rather earn your trust with a transparent quote than a fake review.
