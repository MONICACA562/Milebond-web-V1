Getting Started

New to working with a sourcing agent? Here's what to expect.

We don't just match you with the first supplier we find. We start from our own pool of several thousand pre-vetted suppliers across China, then cross-check against Alibaba listings and the Yiwu market for multi-way comparison. For online sellers, we actively encourage you to bring your own ideas — product tweaks, custom features, packaging concepts. We validate whether they're feasible, who can produce them, and at what cost. You're not buying off-the-shelf; you're building something with us.

1) Send product details (image, ASIN, target price, MOQ). 2) Free analysis with 3 supplier comparisons and price benchmarks. 3) Approve a supplier. 4) We arrange samples. 5) After sample approval, bulk order and QC to shipment.

Alibaba is a starting point, not a final destination. 70%+ of "factories" on Alibaba are trading companies. We verify actual factory status via the raw material cost test, negotiate MOQ reductions (often 50-70%), and optimize packaging for FBA. If you're already profitable, our savings typically cover our fee.

Depends on your stage. If you're a startup focused on marketing and sales, stay where you are — sourcing, QC, and shipping are fully handled by us locally. If you're an established seller looking for new product ideas, visiting Yiwu Market is highly recommended: walking through different materials, fabrics, and finishes sparks product combinations you'd never find on Alibaba. We'll guide you through the market.

Yes. You receive the supplier's original quotation and proforma invoice directly. We never mark up product prices or add hidden line items. Our fee is transparently stated as a separate line. MOQ & Customization

Improving supplier cash flow and reducing your inventory backlog — aligned with market reality.

1) Multi-supplier comparison — in Yiwu, 20+ suppliers may make the same product. We find the one willing to do 300. 2) Material pooling — based on your annual forecast, suppliers pre-stock raw material. Real client: MOQ 1000→300, price also dropped 51% because material was mislabeled (201 sold as 304).

Custom logo (laser, printed, engraved) from MOQ 300. Custom packaging depends on complexity — simple box at MOQ 500, full custom at MOQ 1000. We also optimize packaging dimensions to reduce FBA storage and dimensional weight fees.

Based on our clients' actual order volumes, we've successfully reduced MOQ by up to 90% (e.g., from 1000 units down to 300). The method depends on your product: multi-supplier comparison for stock items, material pooling for custom items, and annual volume commitment for suppliers. If a supplier genuinely can't go lower, we'll tell you directly rather than overpromising.

Yes. FNSKU labels, suffocation labels, poly-bag or box per Amazon requirements, DDP shipping to FBA warehouses. We generate shipment labels and packing lists. You just send the Amazon shipment plan.

Yes — one of our most impactful optimizations. We calculate dimensional weight and redesign packaging with Yiwu suppliers to reduce volume 20-40% while protecting the product. Client example: saved $0.80/unit in FBA storage and dimensional shipping.

We verify certification before production, not after Amazon rejects your listing. If a supplier claims compliance but the bulk order fails testing, we negotiate a refund with the supplier and immediately switch to a qualified alternative so you don't run out of stock. We also recommend printing a batch ID on every unit's label for traceability — if a single batch has issues, you isolate it without recalling your entire inventory.

Sending an ASIN is a great starting point, but we don't recommend copying a listing 1:1 — you'll have no competitive advantage and may risk IP issues. Quality & Compliance

Material verification, factory audits, and inspection.

60-70% of "304" in Yiwu is actually 201. Three methods: 1) Test solution on scraped surface — 304 stays, 201 turns red. 2) XRF spectrometer for exact Cr/Ni composition. 3) Salt spray test (48h) for rust resistance. We test on production samples, not just approval samples. Read our full 304 verification guide →

Yes. Third-party AQL 2.5 inspection before balance payment. Critical defects (rust, food safety) = 0 tolerance. You receive an inspection report with photos before we release payment. If supplier is in Yiwu, local inspection starts as low as $100 per order.

Raw material cost test: for stainless steel, raw material should be 55-70% of total cost. We ask for the material purchase invoice — a real factory shows it with their name; a trading company can't. Also verify: business license scope, on-site equipment, and whether they produce only one material type.

FDA, LFGB, EU 1935/2004, California Prop 65, CE, RoHS, REACH, UKCA. We identify the right certification for your product and target market before production, and connect you with our partnered authoritative testing labs in Yiwu. Testing fees depend on product and certification required. We don't sell fake certificates.

Payment & Cash Flow

Protecting your deposit and improving cash flow.

Standard: 30% deposit, 70% before shipment. After 2-3 successful orders, we can negotiate Net 15-45 — you pay after goods arrive. Real client: payment changed from 30/70 to Net 30, improving cash flow cycle by 45 days.

You pay deposit directly to the supplier's verified company account (we verify business license). We never ask you to pay us the deposit then forward it. After inspection passes, you pay balance directly. If there's a quality dispute, we mediate — but funds never pass through our operating account.

Supplier payments: T/T bank transfer. Our service fee: T/T, PayPal, Wise. We can also arrange Alibaba Trade Assurance for the supplier order (adds ~2-3% cost).

Avoiding Pitfalls

What we see every week in Yiwu market.

Material fraud — 201 sold as 304. Supplier sends genuine 304 sample for approval, then uses 201 for mass production. You discover it when customers complain about rust after 3 months. We test on production samples, not just approval samples. Read our 6 Yiwu scams guide →

Warning signs: 1) Always recommends same 1-2 suppliers, refuses alternatives. 2) Prices 10-20% above market. 3) Fee is "free" or suspiciously low. 4) You've never seen a supplier invoice directly. Read our kickback detection guide →

Canton Fair: established suppliers, strong English, high MOQ (500-1000+), 30/70 payment. Yiwu: 75,000+ suppliers in one building, lower MOQ (300), flexible packaging, but limited English. Most Canton Fair suppliers actually source from Yiwu. Read our full comparison →

We include delivery timeline and penalty clauses in the purchase contract. For quality issues, we inspect before shipment — if first-time pass rate drops, we halt shipment and require rework before paying balance. You never pay for defective goods. If supplier can't fix it, we find an alternative.
