EU Compliance Checklist

Full compliance support for selling snuffle mats into the EU / UK market.

  • REACH (EC 1907/2006): azo dyes, cadmium, lead, phthalates (DEHP / DBP / BBP) limits
  • Textile labelling: EU 1007/2011 fibre composition labelling
  • Toy safety: EN 71-1 (small parts / sharp edges) and EN 71-3 (migratable elements)
  • Food-contact materials: EC 1935/2004 (direct contact with dog food)
  • CE marking under Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC
  • Choking-hazard warning label (small parts / loose-thread risk)
  • UKCA marking for the UK market
